Privacy Policy for Crypto News

Effective Date: November 24, 2026
Package Name: com.aistudio.cryptonews.hqswrz

This Privacy Policy explains how Crypto News ("the App") handles information when you use our Android application. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency about how the App operates.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Crypto News does not require account registration, login, or personal profile creation. The developer does not operate backend servers that collect, store, or sell your name, email address, phone number, wallet addresses, or personal financial data.

2. Local Data Stored on Your Device

To provide fast offline-first browsing and customizable alerts, the App stores the following preferences and cached content strictly locally on your device:

  • Cached cryptocurrency news articles and macro-economic/airdrop calendar events (Room local database).
  • Your bookmarked Watchlist events.
  • App preferences such as selected language, timezone, Bitcoin (BTC) price alert status, alert threshold percentage, and reference baseline BTC price (SharedPreferences).

This local data stays on your device and is removed if you clear the App's storage or uninstall the App.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Collection

The App connects to third-party services that may receive standard network request data (such as IP address) or device identifiers according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Used to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect and process your Android Advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, app interaction signals, and crash/diagnostic metrics to serve and measure ads. You can manage ad personalization or reset your Advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Learn more at Google Privacy & Terms.
  • CryptoCompare API & CoinGecko API: Used to fetch public cryptocurrency news articles and live market prices. No personal user identifiers are intentionally sent by the App to these market data providers.

4. Device Permissions

  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Required to fetch live crypto news, market prices, and display advertisements.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Optional runtime permission on Android 13+ used solely to deliver Watchlist event reminders and Bitcoin price fluctuation alerts that you enable.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Used to trigger local reminder notifications 30 minutes before Watchlist events you choose to follow.

5. External Links

News articles, event sources, and sponsored promotions may open external websites in your device browser. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

6. Children's Privacy

Crypto News is intended for general audiences interested in cryptocurrency market news and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
